# Photos

Drop images in here and they appear on the site. No code change needed — the
page reads this folder at build time.

| File | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| `avatar.jpg` | The badge in the bottom-right of the hero. A square headshot works best. |
| anything else | The gallery in the Reach section, sorted by filename |

So `01-finish.jpg`, `02-bike.jpg`, `03-swim.jpg` control the gallery order.

**What to include.** Six to ten shots that answer "who is this and can he
actually do it": a finish line, something on the bike, open water, a portrait,
and anything with a crowd or a race number in it. Landscape crops survive the
gallery better than portrait ones.

**Size.** 2000px on the long edge is plenty. JPEG or WebP. Keep each under
~600 KB; Next will serve resized versions from these.

Anything already public on Instagram is fine to reuse, but the originals off
your phone or from the photographer will look considerably better than what
Instagram serves back.
